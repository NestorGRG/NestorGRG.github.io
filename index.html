About

Postdoctoral Researcher

Theoretical Chemistry researcher at the University of Barcelona.

  • Website: nestorgrg.github.io
  • University: University of Barcelona
  • City: Barcelona, Spain
  • Degree: Ph.D. in Theoretical Chemistry
  • Email: nestorgarcia-romeral@ub.edu

My main field is the application of ab initio computational methods to investigate and predict the physical properties of advanced solid-state materials. Driven by a deep interest in two-dimensional systems, my current focus areas include:

  • Electronic Structure of 2D Materials: Property modulation and electronic characterization of MXenes
  • Solid-State Magnetism: Modeling of magnetic exchange interactions in 2D materials
  • Surface Spectroscopy Modeling: Computational modeling of surface XPS core-level shifts
  • Machine Learning Force Fields: Development of ML-based potentials for large-scale simulations
